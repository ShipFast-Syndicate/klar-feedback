# Code of conduct

This tracker exists so people can say what's broken. That works when it's a decent place
to be.

**Expected:** be direct about the product, considerate about the people. Bug reports can
be blunt — "this is broken and here's why" is welcome and useful. Say what you actually
experienced rather than what you assume we did wrong.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, deliberately
wasting people's time, and posting other people's private information.

**If something crosses the line**, email `klar-support@alphabros.eu`. Reports go to the
maintainers and stay between us and you.

Maintainers may edit, lock or delete issues and comments that break this, and may block
accounts that keep doing it. We'd much rather not.
