# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue.** Email **`klar-support@alphabros.eu`** with:

- what you found, and the URL or endpoint it affects
- the steps to reproduce it
- what an attacker could do with it

We'll confirm we've received it and tell you what we're doing about it. If you'd like
credit once it's fixed, say so and we'll name you; if you'd rather stay anonymous, that's
fine too.

We're a small team, so we won't promise a response time we can't keep. What we will do is
answer honestly about whether and when we're fixing something.

## Scope

In scope: `klar.alphabros.eu` and the tracker script served from it.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised device or browser extension, reports
generated by a scanner with no demonstrated impact, and anything that needs physical
access to a machine we don't control.

## Please don't

- Run automated scans that degrade the service for other people.
- Access, modify or exfiltrate data belonging to anyone but yourself. If you stumble into
  someone else's data, stop and tell us — that's the finding.
- Publish before we've had a chance to fix it.

## What Klar collects, for context

Klar stores no cookies and no personal data. A visitor identifier is derived server-side
from a daily-rotating salted hash and cannot be linked across days; raw IP addresses and
user-agent strings are used to compute it and are never stored. If you find something
that contradicts that, it's exactly what we want to hear about.
